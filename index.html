Server Rules

Respect the family and keep the community clean.

RULE 01

Respect Everyone

Treat every member with respect. Harassment, insults and discrimination are not allowed.

RULE 02

No Spam

Avoid spam, message flooding and unnecessary mentions.

RULE 03

No Advertising

Advertising other servers, websites or services without permission is not allowed.

RULE 04

Keep Channels Clean

Use each channel for its intended purpose and keep conversations organized.

RULE 05

Listen to Staff

Follow the instructions of the staff team and respect moderation decisions.

RULE 06

Have Fun

Enjoy the community, meet new people and represent Russian Family positively.
